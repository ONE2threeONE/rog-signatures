Michael Kraus: email signature
1. Click Copy signature (or click the signature and press Cmd+A, then Cmd+C).
2. In Gmail: Settings, See all settings, General, Signature. Delete the old one, click + Create new, click in the box, press Cmd+V.
3. Set both dropdowns to it, Save Changes, reload Gmail.
